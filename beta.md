## What You’ll Need

Before getting started, make sure you have:

- An **iPhone or iPad**
- An **Apple ID**
- The **TestFlight app** installed from the App Store

---

## Step-by-Step: How to Join the Beta

### 1. Install TestFlight
Download **TestFlight** from the App Store if you don’t already have it.

---

### 2. Accept the Beta Invitation
Use the TestFlight invite link provided by RecoveryFit.

> Once you tap the link, TestFlight will open automatically.

---

### 3. Install RecoveryFit
Inside TestFlight:

- Tap **Install**
- Wait for the app to download
- Open RecoveryFit like any other app

---

### 4. Keep Auto-Updates On (Recommended)

Beta builds are updated frequently. In TestFlight:

- Tap **RecoveryFit**
- Enable **Automatic Updates**

This ensures you always have the latest version.

---

## Providing Feedback (Very Important)

Beta feedback helps improve RecoveryFit for everyone. You can:

- Report bugs directly through **TestFlight**
- Share feedback inside the app (if available)
- Email us directly at:  
  📧 **support@recoveryfitapp.com**

When reporting an issue, please include:

- What you were doing
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened

---

## Important Notes About Beta Builds

- Beta versions may contain **bugs or unfinished features**
- Performance may vary between builds
- Data may change or reset between updates

If you need maximum stability, use the public App Store version instead.

---

## Leaving the Beta

You can leave the beta at any time:

- Open **TestFlight**
- Select **RecoveryFit**
- Tap **Stop Testing**
- Delete the app from your device

---

Thanks for helping make RecoveryFit better 💪
