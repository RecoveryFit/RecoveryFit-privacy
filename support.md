# Support & FAQ

Welcome to **RecoveryFit Support**.  
Find answers to common questions and guidance on using the app effectively.

</div>

<div class="section">

## Getting Started

<details>
<summary>How do I add a workout?</summary>
Tap a date on the calendar and select **Add Workout**. You can log a past workout or schedule one for later.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I edit a workout after completing it?</summary>
Yes. Completed workouts can be edited from your workout history.
</details>

</div>

<div class="section">

## Workouts & Training

<details>
<summary>Can I run multiple workouts at the same time?</summary>
No. RecoveryFit allows only one active workout session at a time to preserve accurate tracking.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I close the app during a workout?</summary>
Your workout remains active. Reopening the app will restore the session.
</details>

</div>

<div class="section">

## Recovery & Metrics

<details>
<summary>How is recovery calculated?</summary>
Recovery is calculated using workout load, intensity, and recovery-focused sessions.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does RecoveryFit sync with Apple Health?</summary>
Yes. RecoveryFit integrates with Apple Health for supported metrics.
</details>

</div>
