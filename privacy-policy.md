# Privacy Policy for RecoveryFit

**Last Updated:** March 2026

RecoveryFit (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how RecoveryFit (owned and operated by Clayton Stevens) collects, uses, and protects data when you use the RecoveryFit mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Workout & Fitness Data

RecoveryFit collects fitness-related data that you voluntarily enter or generate while using the app.

This includes:
- Workout logs and exercise history
- Sets, reps, weight, duration, distance
- Recovery sessions and recovery scores (0–100)
- Muscle fatigue and recovery metrics
- Custom exercises and routines
- Heart rate data (manual entry or via integrations)
- Calories, workout duration, intensity
- Recovery impact scores

---

### B. Health & Recovery Data

RecoveryFit may collect health-related data, either manually entered by you or read from connected health services (with permission):

- Sleep duration and quality
- Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
- Resting heart rate
- Muscle soreness levels
- Stress, mood, and energy levels
- Hydration levels
- Height and weight

---

### C. Profile & Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

- Name (optional)
- Email address
- Age or date of birth (optional)
- Gender (optional)
- Height, weight, and fitness preferences
- App settings and preferences

---

### D. Device & App Usage Data

When enabled, RecoveryFit collects limited technical and usage data to improve app performance:

- Screen views and navigation events
- Feature usage
- App lifecycle events
- Performance metrics
- Crash reports and error logs

This data does **not include detailed workout or health values**.

---

### E. Purchase & Subscription Data

If you purchase a subscription, transaction data is processed through Apple and RevenueCat. We do not collect or store full payment details.

We may receive:
- Subscription status (active/inactive)
- Product identifiers
- Purchase and expiration dates

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

- Provide core app functionality
- Calculate recovery and fatigue metrics
- Sync data across devices (if enabled)
- Manage subscriptions and access to premium features
- Improve app performance and stability
- Identify bugs and crashes
- Enhance user experience

We do **not** use your data for:
- Advertising
- Selling personal data
- Third-party marketing

---

## 3. Data Storage

RecoveryFit uses a combination of **local device storage** and **secure cloud storage**.

- Workout and health data may be stored locally and/or synced to secure cloud services (e.g., Firebase)
- Account-related data is stored securely in the cloud
- We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data

---

## 4. Analytics & Crash Reporting

Analytics and crash reporting may be enabled to improve app quality.

### What is collected:
- App performance data
- Error and crash logs
- General usage patterns

### What is NOT collected:
- Sensitive health values in analytics tools
- Full workout details
- Payment information

### User Control:
You can disable analytics and crash reporting at any time from **Profile → Privacy Settings**.

---

## 5. Health Integrations

RecoveryFit can read data from:

- **Apple Health**
- **Google Fit**

Only with your explicit permission.

We only access data necessary to provide app functionality.

RecoveryFit:
- Does **not sell** health data
- Does **not use health data for advertising**
- Does **not share health data with third parties**

We do not write data back to Apple Health unless explicitly stated.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not sell or rent** your personal data.

We may share limited data with trusted service providers strictly for app functionality, such as:

- Cloud storage providers (e.g., Firebase)
- Analytics and crash reporting tools
- Subscription infrastructure (e.g., RevenueCat)

These providers only process data on our behalf and are required to protect it.

---

## 7. Your Rights & Choices

You have the ability to:

- Access and update your account information
- Disable analytics and crash reporting
- Revoke health data permissions (Apple Health / Google Fit)
- Delete your account (if applicable)

Deleting your account will remove associated cloud data, subject to reasonable retention for legal and operational purposes.

---

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the Service.

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable timeframe unless required by law to retain it.

---

## 9. Children’s Privacy

RecoveryFit is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. If we make material changes, we will notify users through the app or other appropriate means.

---
